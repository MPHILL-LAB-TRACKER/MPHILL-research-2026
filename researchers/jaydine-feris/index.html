Ms Jaydine Feris

Postgraduate student · MSc in Biomedical Sciences

Biography & research

Ms Jaydine Feris is listed among the postgraduate students under capacity building in Biomedical Sciences. Her research investigates antibiotic-resistance patterns, molecular detection of resistance genes, and nanoparticle-based antimicrobial activity against oral and gastrointestinal bacterial pathogens.
