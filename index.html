Research capabilities

The science behind our laboratory

Advanced 3D Cell Culture

Our laboratory utilizes state-of-the-art 3D culture systems that closely mimic the natural tissue environment, enabling more accurate studies of tissue regeneration and cellular behavior.

Biomaterial Development

We focus on the creation of biocompatible materials that support cell growth and tissue integration, ensuring effective and durable healing processes.

Regenerative therapeutics

Our research encompasses a wide range of regenerative therapies aimed at restoring function and alleviating damage caused by injury or disease.

Precision in Drug Delivery

We develop cutting-edge nanoparticle systems designed to enhance bioavailability and targeted delivery of therapeutics, improving outcomes in various medical applications.

Microscale platform

Our micro-scale delivery platforms are engineered to release drugs in a controlled manner, allowing for sustained therapeutic effects while minimizing side effects.

Understanding Interactions

We investigate how different materials influence cell adhesion, proliferation, and differentiation, providing valuable insights for tissue engineering applications.

Material selection

We explore various biomaterials to determine their compatibility with specific cell types, ensuring optimal integration and performance in regenerative applications.

2D vs 3D Dynamic

Our research includes comparative studies of cell behavior in 2D and 3D environments, revealing crucial differences that impact tissue regeneration outcomes.

Functional Outcome

By analyzing cell-material interactions, we aim to predict functional outcomes of tissue engineering strategies, enhancing the effectiveness of our regenerative approaches.

Material development

We focus on the synthesis of biocompatible scaffolds that support cell adhesion and growth, serving as a foundation for tissue regeneration.

Design innovations

Our team implements innovative designs in scaffold architecture to optimize nutrient flow and cellular interaction, crucial for effective tissue repair.

Application in medicine

The scaffolds and nanomaterials we develop are applied across various medical fields, including orthopedics, wound healing, Gastric complications and dental applications, demonstrating their versatility.
