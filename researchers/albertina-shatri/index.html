Biography & research

Dr Albertina Shatri is the Project Principal Investigator in the CRP–ICGEB research grant collaboration. The team presentation places her in the School of Medicine at the University of Namibia.

CRP–ICGEB research grant collaboration: project leadership across the named University of Namibia and University of Pretoria team.
