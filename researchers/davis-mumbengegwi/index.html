Biography & research

Prof Davis Mumbengegwi is listed in the CRP–ICGEB research grant collaboration at the Centre for Research Services, University of Namibia. His selected publisher-checked work addresses malaria epidemiology and molecular detection in Namibia.

Malaria risk, infection and detection: The selected articles examine epidemiological risk factors, parasite-species detection and low-density infections. They document research coauthorship; they are not automatically classified as TED² laboratory outputs.
