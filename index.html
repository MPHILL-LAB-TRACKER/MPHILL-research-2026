Research capabilities

From cellular models to translational platforms.

TED² brings together tissue-engineering and drug-development approaches to study biological behaviour, material performance and therapeutic delivery.

3D

Advanced 3D Cell Culture

State-of-the-art 3D culture systems are used to more closely mimic natural tissue environments and support studies of tissue regeneration and cellular behaviour.

BM

Biomaterial Development

Development of biocompatible materials that support cell growth and tissue integration for effective and durable regenerative applications.

RT

Regenerative Therapeutics

Research into regenerative strategies intended to restore function and address tissue damage associated with injury or disease.

DD

Precision in Drug Delivery

Nanoparticle-based systems are explored to improve therapeutic bioavailability and targeted delivery in biomedical applications.

µ

Microscale Platforms

Microscale delivery platforms are designed for controlled and sustained release of therapeutics while aiming to reduce undesirable effects.

↔

Cell–Material Interactions

Investigation of how materials affect cell adhesion, proliferation and differentiation to inform tissue-engineering strategies.

Material selection Compatibility between biomaterials and specific cell types.
2D vs 3D dynamics Comparative analysis of cellular behaviour across model systems.
Functional outcomes Linking cell–material interactions to regenerative performance.
Design innovation Scaffold architecture, nutrient flow and cellular integration.
