Biography & research

Associate Professor at the University of Namibia. Her public researcher profiles describe work in mycology, medicinal plants and drug discovery, including research on Namibian Ganoderma mushrooms.

Selected published work examines Ganoderma mushrooms, phenolic composition and in vitro antioxidant activity, and antiplasmodial activity of Namibian medicinal-plant extracts.
