Ms Naungwe Simasiku

Postgraduate student · MSc in Medical Microbiology

Biography & research

Ms Naungwe Simasiku is listed among the postgraduate students under capacity building, in the MSc in Medical Microbiology programme. Her research concerns bacteria associated with acute dental abscesses and their susceptibility to antibiotics and novel plant-based nanoparticles.

The Isolation and Identification of Bacteria Causing Acute Dental Abscesses in Adults Admitted To The Katutura Dental Facilities and Evaluating Their Susceptibility to Antibiotics And Novel Plant-based Nanoparticles
